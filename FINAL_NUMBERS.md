## Core Conclusion Metrics
- The Wavelet-Copula model **underestimated 99% VaR** by approximately **35%** relative to the historical benchmark (-1.167% vs -1.806%).
- This underestimation led to a severe failure in out-of-sample backtesting, with **28 violations** where only ~7 were expected (a rejection of the Kupiec test at p < 1e-8).
- **Finding:** While tail dependence clearly changes across horizons (e.g., SPY-GLD dependence doubles from short to long), treating wavelet horizons as statistically independent during simulation destroys the cross-horizon volatility clustering that characterizes real market crashes.
